# Theme 04 — Moonlit Bamboo (月白)
*Monochrome black-ink + silver-white bamboo grove under a full moon. The most literary / minimalist of the four.*

## Concept
Pure black-ink shuimo with cool moonlit silver-white undertones. Tall straight bamboo stalks dominate the left, a pale silver moon glows in the upper-right, calligraphic leaves painted with confident strokes, a single stone lantern in the middle distance. The most contemplative theme — ideal for serious writers who want zero distraction and a meditative late-night writing atmosphere.

## Implementation Tips
- This is the only theme that works equally well in light mode and dark mode — in dark mode, just invert the overlay to rgba(26,26,26,0.55).
- A single accent color works best: choose either the cool silver #C8D0DC or a single warm lantern glow #E8B36A for the primary CTA — never both.
- The Chinese name 月白 (yuebai) literally means "moon white" — a classical color term for a faint cool blue-white. Append 月白, 墨竹, 留白, 水墨 for stronger cultural anchoring if the model supports it.
