# Theme 03 — Vermilion Maple (丹枫)
*Warm autumn shuimo — cinnabar red, burnt sienna, and pale gold. A melancholic poetic mood for late-night writing sessions.*

## Concept
An autumn ink-wash landscape in warm vermilion and burnt-sienna monochrome. Rolling hills, a curving stream of pale gold, red maple branches cascading from the upper corner, a thatched-roof cottage on a distant ridge, a few wild geese flying in V-formation. The warmest of the four themes — great for romance, period drama, or melancholic literary fiction.

Xuan paper texture with visible calligraphic brush strokes, no text, no UI, no watermark, no signature, no logo. Poetic, melancholic, autumnal, ultra detailed, 8K, panoramic 16:9 composition, warm autumn late-afternoon lighting, atmospheric perspective.

- The maple branch in the upper-right naturally frames the login card.

## Implementation Tips
- Pair with a warm dark surface for cards (e.g. rgba(42, 20, 16, 0.85)) so the cream text glows against it.
- Use the cinnabar red #B23A2C sparingly — reserve for the primary CTA and active sidebar item only.
- The Chinese name 丹枫 literally means "cinnabar maple" — append 秋日山水, 丹枫, 茅庐, 飞雁 for stronger cultural anchoring if the model supports it.
